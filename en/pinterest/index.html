How to download a video from Pinterest

Downloading a supported Pinterest video with NoMark is straightforward. The bot automatically detects the Pinterest link, so you do not need to select the platform manually.

  1. Open Pinterest and find the Pin containing the video.
  2. Tap Share and copy the Pinterest link.
  3. Open the NoMark Telegram bot.
  4. Send the copied Pinterest URL to the bot.
  5. Wait while NoMark processes the link and prepares the video.
