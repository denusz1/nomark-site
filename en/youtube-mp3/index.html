YouTube to MP3 converter

NoMark is a simple Telegram-based tool for converting supported regular YouTube video links into MP3 audio. Instead of opening a separate converter website, you can copy the YouTube link and send it directly to the NoMark Telegram bot.

How to download MP3 from YouTube

The process is designed to be simple. NoMark automatically detects a supported regular YouTube link and processes it as audio, so there is no need to choose the platform or MP3 mode manually.

  1. Open the regular YouTube video you want to use.
  2. Tap Share and copy the video URL.
  3. Open the NoMark Telegram bot.
  4. Send the copied YouTube link to the bot.
  5. Wait while NoMark processes the link and prepares the MP3 audio file.

Convert YouTube videos to MP3

NoMark's regular YouTube workflow is focused on audio. When you send a supported regular YouTube video link, the bot prepares the result as an MP3 file instead of a video download.

YouTube MP3 downloader in Telegram

NoMark works directly through Telegram, which keeps the conversion process simple. You do not need to install another application or search for a different online converter every time. Send the link to the bot and receive the result in the same Telegram conversation.

Why use NoMark for YouTube audio?

The main advantage is convenience. Once you have the YouTube URL, you only need to send it to NoMark. The bot automatically recognizes the supported regular YouTube link and handles it as an MP3 task.

YouTube Shorts and regular YouTube videos

NoMark treats Shorts differently from regular YouTube videos. Supported YouTube Shorts links are processed as video, while supported regular YouTube links use the MP3 workflow described on this page.

Can I convert every YouTube video?

NoMark processes supported regular YouTube links. Availability can depend on the particular URL and content being requested, so some links may not be processed successfully.
