Download videos and audio with NoMark

NoMark is a simple Telegram-based downloader for supported content from popular platforms. Instead of opening a complicated website, you can simply copy a link and send it to the NoMark Telegram bot.

TikTok video downloader

NoMark can process supported TikTok links and provide the video without the original TikTok watermark. This makes it convenient to save a video for personal use when you need a clean copy of supported content.

YouTube Shorts downloader

YouTube Shorts links are handled separately from regular YouTube videos. Send a supported Shorts link to NoMark and the bot prepares the video for you.

Pinterest video downloader

Found a useful or interesting video on Pinterest? Copy the supported Pinterest link and send it to NoMark in Telegram. The bot automatically recognizes the platform and prepares the video.

YouTube to MP3

Regular YouTube videos are processed as audio. NoMark can convert supported YouTube links into MP3, making it easier to listen to the audio without keeping the full video.

Why use a Telegram downloader?

A Telegram bot keeps the process simple. There is no need to install another application, choose a platform manually or search for a separate downloader every time. Copy the link, send it to NoMark and let the bot determine what to do with it.

How to download a video with NoMark

  1. Copy a supported TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Pinterest link.
  2. Open the NoMark bot in Telegram.
  3. Send the copied link to the bot.
  4. Wait while NoMark processes the link and prepares the result.

For regular YouTube links, the result is provided as MP3 audio instead of a video file.
